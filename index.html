    Claude vision assistant (optional)

    Sends one still frame per visit to Claude to write a plain-language description and, when the camera is unsure, to judge which household member the visible attributes fit (age group, height, hair, build, clothing, notes). Claude never identifies people by their face. Frames go to Anthropic's API under your key; nothing is stored in this site.

    Browser storage

    People, events and settings live in this browser's local storage; clips and snapshots in its IndexedDB. Nothing leaves your computer. Clearing site data or switching browsers starts from scratch, so export a backup now and then. Clips are downloaded one by one from the Events tab.
